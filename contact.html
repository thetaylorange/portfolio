Hello! I am a Graphic and UX Designer. I am based in Chattanooga, Tennessee where I graduated Summa Cum Laude and received my BFA in 2020, with a concentration in Graphic Design.

I have over 3 years of design experience working in both agency and in-house settings, primarily focused on designing for both web and mobile applications. I have worked on Agile and cross-functional teams, which has granted me to gain insight into Digital Marketing practices and the ability to create both written and visual content. In addition to design skills I am adept at copy editing and brainstorming, auditing websites in order to ensure that they are upholding company values and customer needs, logotypes, and research involving customer experience and brand consistency.

I aim to make work that is well-informed and holistic. I inform and engage my audience and apply the knowledge gained through personal and collective experience. I often inform my work with feminist theory, the history of hand crafts, and the social impact of design. I am interested in the ways people engage with visual ephemera and digital culture. I have been exploring themes of memory, identity, and the act of recounting personal experience visually. My work is focused on expanding the meaning and intention of design from a historical view, and working to push the lines of what is design, what is productive, and what is universal.

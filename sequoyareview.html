Sequoya Review

2020

The Sequoya Review is an annual collection of student writing and artwork. I was the Design Editor for the 2020 edition and was in charge of selecting artwork from student submissions, laying out the publication, and seeking out the cover artwork for the SR. Originally designed for print publication, the SR had to abruptly shift to a digital format due to the COVID-19 outbreak. A big thank you to Quin Crumb for assisting, Kat Friedmann for her exquisite work, and Matt Greenwell for advising.

Design Editor: Taylor A. Johnson

Assistant Design Editor: Quin Crumb

Cover Artwork: Kat Friedmann
